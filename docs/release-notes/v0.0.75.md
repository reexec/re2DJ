# re2DJ v0.0.75

## 변경 사항

- 화면 업스케일 셰이더 셋을 내장했습니다. `--post-shader` 옵션이나 백틱(`` ` ``) OSD의 Screen shader에서 고릅니다. 모두 프레임 하나만 보며, 창이 클수록(특히 4K 전체 화면) `none`보다 선명합니다.
  - `fsr`: AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 1.0. 경계 방향을 따라 키우는 EASU와 선명하게 하는 RCAS 두 단계입니다. 사진·CG 전반에 어울리고, `FSR_SHARPNESS` 슬라이더로 선명도를 조절합니다.
  - `scalefx`: Sp00kyFox의 ScaleFX. 픽셀아트용이라 EZ2DJ의 CG와 안티앨리어싱 글자는 윤곽이 딱딱해지고 배경이 단색화됩니다. 비교용으로 넣었습니다.
  - `anime4k`: bloc97의 Anime4K Upscale CNN x2 (S). 작은 신경망이 윤곽 세부를 더해 잡티 없이 적당히 선명합니다.
- 화면 셰이더가 여러 pass를 잇습니다. `shaders/` 폴더에 libretro 형식의 `.glslp` preset(부분집합)을 두면 내장 셰이더처럼 고를 수 있습니다. 형식은 [가이드](../guides/post-process-shaders.md)에 있습니다.
- 4th 4K 전체 화면에서 vsync를 끈 처리량(RTX 4090): `none` 799, `fsr` 688, `scalefx` 538, `anime4k` 565fps. 60fps 기본 설정에서는 모두 여유가 큽니다. 스팀덱처럼 약한 GPU에서는 재지 않았습니다.

## English

# re2DJ v0.0.75

## Changes

- Three screen upscaling shaders are built in, chosen with `--post-shader` or the Screen shader item of the backtick (`` ` ``) OSD. All look at one frame, and the larger the window (fullscreen at 4K above all) the sharper they are than `none`.
  - `fsr`: AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 1.0, two passes: EASU scaling along edge directions and RCAS sharpening. It suits photos and CG in general, with the `FSR_SHARPNESS` slider for the strength.
  - `scalefx`: ScaleFX by Sp00kyFox. Made for pixel art, it gives EZ2DJ's CG and anti-aliased lettering hard outlines and posterized backgrounds; included for comparison.
  - `anime4k`: Anime4K Upscale CNN x2 (S) by bloc97, a small network adding edge detail, moderately sharper without artifacts.
- Screen shaders chain passes: a libretro-style `.glslp` preset (a subset of the format) in `shaders/` can be chosen like a built-in. The format is in the [guide](../guides/post-process-shaders.md).
- 4th fullscreen at 4K with vsync off (RTX 4090): `none` 799, `fsr` 688, `scalefx` 538, `anime4k` 565 fps, all with ample headroom at the 60 fps default; weaker GPUs such as a Steam Deck's were not measured.

## Validation

- Windows x86 Debug (6,519 unit checks, 0 failures, 4 CTest tests), Release, the GL probe's 12 checks, and WSL Linux x64, x86 and clang (warnings as errors, 5 CTest tests each)
- On Windows fullscreen at 4K, 4th and 6th captured on the same scene under `none` and the three shaders and compared; real runs of a user `.glslp` and of a single-pass shader reading `OrigTexture`
- A five-angle review with per-finding refutation before the commit, its 11 confirmed findings fixed (a failed FBO target failing the whole presentation, the `OrigTexture` regression in single-pass shaders, and more)

Original game executables, HDD data, CHD images and Hardlock material (`cfg/`) are not included in release packages. The built-in shaders' licenses (AMD FSR 1, ScaleFX, Anime4K, all MIT) are in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
